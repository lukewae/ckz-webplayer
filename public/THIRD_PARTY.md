Duck follow-up, 10 October 2026: CKZ's pinned RemoveCrouchJumpBind/ReduceDuckSlowdown rules informed the input and speed handling. The user-supplied [Kisak-Strike CS movement reference](https://github.com/SwagSoftware/Kisak-Strike/blob/master/game/shared/cstrike15/cs_gamemovement.cpp) was inspected for behavioral comparison; no source copied verbatim. This is not a public CS2 engine implementation or a parity oracle. See docs/DUCK-MECHANICS.md for assumptions and measurement differences.

[Valve's public Source SDK LadderMove](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013/blob/master/src/game/shared/gamemovement.cpp) was inspected on 9 October 2026 as a behavioral reference for view-space ladder projection, per-axis input, contact retention and outward jump-off. The small vector formulation in `src/physics/ladder.ts` was written independently. This older engine reference does not establish CS2 parity; CKZ ladder scale/dampening values come from the pinned Classic header above.
